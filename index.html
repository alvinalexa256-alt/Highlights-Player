What this tool generates

Each card uses your exact containerMatch HTML — same format already on your Blogger posts. Logos come from ESPN. Channels come from your Firebase livetv/channels.

Two ways to publish

Option A — Static HTML: Copy from ⟨/⟩ HTML tab, paste into a Blogger post in HTML mode.

Option B — Firebase Widget: Push to Firebase once, paste the widget (📡 tab) once into your Blogger theme. Done — just push new matches daily.

Channel matching

Reads your livetv/channels from Firebase and fuzzy-matches ESPN broadcast names to your channel names. Click any chip to override.

Merge vs Replace

Merge — adds to existing data. Good for adding more matches later in the day.
Replace — clears first. Use at start of each day.
